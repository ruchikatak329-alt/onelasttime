A Little About Me

I'm a social media manager and content-focused creative who loves turning simple ideas into content that actually gets noticed. Most recently, I worked with Catchy Climbers Marketing Agency, where I handled a client's social media page end to end  planning their content calendar, building post and reel concepts, and shaping a voice that felt consistent across every platform.

Alongside strategy and management, I also edit video  cutting raw footage into short, polished pieces built to hold attention. I enjoy sitting at the intersection of planning and making: thinking through the strategy, then actually building the content myself.

For me, good social media is where creativity, strategy and storytelling meet  and that combination is what I bring to every brand I work with.

good content is a little bit of magic and a lot of intention.

IndustryCafe
Platform Instagram

Goal

The goal was to build an online presence that felt as inviting as the cafe itself  a warm, aesthetic space that drew in both loyal locals and curious newcomers. Inc objective was to increase brand awareness, highlight the cafe's cozy atmosphere. and encourage foot traffic through a consistent, engaging content strategy.

Approach

I designed a cohesive feed using soft brown tones, natural textures, and curated imagery to reflect the cafe's earthy, welcoming vibe. The content balanced beautifully lit flat lays, behind the scenes customer shots. and UG style snippets to bring a lived in, authentic feel. I planned content pillars around menu spotlights. seasonal features, community storytelling, and cafe culture - all tailored to connect emotionally with the audience and turn casual scrollers into regulars.

Please note: this is a passion project — a mock feed. This is not a real cafe.
